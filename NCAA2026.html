Contest Rules

Read carefully before submitting.

1 · Predictions

Pick a winner for each of the 63 games from the Round of 64 onward. Also predict the total combined points scored in the championship game as a tiebreaker.


Your bracket must yield exactly 63 total wins, with one team (your champion) having 6 wins.

2 · Scoring

Your score is the sum over all 64 teams of (predicted wins − actual wins)². A perfect bracket scores 0. Lower is always better.


Example: predict Duke wins 4 games, they actually win 2 → Duke's contribution is (4−2)² = 4.

3 · Winner

  • Participant with lowest score wins.
  • Ties broken by closest championship game point total prediction.

4 · Stakes & Deadlines

  • No entry fee. Winner gets bragging rights which never expire.
  • Men's: noon Thursday March 19, 2026.
  • Women's: noon Friday March 20, 2026.
  • Separate contests, separate leaderboards.
